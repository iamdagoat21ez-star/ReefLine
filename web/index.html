Satellite analysis

Mann–Kendall and Sen’s slope use deseasonalized monthly data. Significant means p < 0.05. Candidate ranking uses 60% normalized slope difference and 40% distance. Candidates may lie outside the selected region.

Hardware architecture & unit economics

Dusk Light Buoy ($145) → downward light at dusk → modeled zooplankton concentration. Bio-Shield Film ($65) → modeled surface shielding and nutrient release. Pi Reef Node ($185) → field temperature and motion-triggered camera records.

Each unit on the slider represents one buoy and one film canister. Pi nodes = ceil(units ÷ 4). At 15 paired units, 15 × $145 + 15 × $65 + 4 × $185 = $3,890. Cost per m² uses a 672 m² sector × selected coverage. The $4,650 reference configuration uses 18 buoys, 20 films and 4 Pi nodes.

The 2.6x growth, 30% UV reduction, 88% survival, species bars, pH/disease overlays and cover forecasts are illustrative model assumptions. Satellite records support the measured trend cards. Unavailable geography or dates are labeled.

Your guide to ReefLine

  1. Choose where to explore.

    Start with Worldwide reef explorer to see all coasts, or zoom into a region. Choose measured or illustrative telemetry with Location data. Use “Inspect a reef location” or click a map marker to see its statistics.

  2. Find an early warning.

    Choose SST or chlorophyll-a and an analysis period. The reference selector lists worsening reefs with p < 0.05.

  3. Compare exactly three candidates.

    Click “Compare 3 reefs” to see reefs with p ≥ 0.05, ranked by 60% slope difference and 40% geographic distance. Measured reefs match measured reefs. Illustrative reefs match illustrative reefs.

  4. Select a Pi deployment site.

    Choose one candidate to view satellite statistics beside the latest station image, receive a Recommended Action, and download the Action Report.

  5. Explore interventions.

    Toggle the three hardware tools and adjust coverage and paired units. The animated reef, budget, map halos and forecast respond together. All biological outcomes and species-cover values are illustrative.

Navigation shortcuts · Ctrl/⌘ K focuses search. Escape closes dialogs. “Reset region view” returns to the current region. Dashed markers and ILLUSTRATIVE badges identify simulated readings. NOAA geography labels distinguish documented reef-region markers from illustrative locations.
